Cookie Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

OntarioDriveTestMap sets exactly one cookie. There is no advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking on this site, so there's nothing here requiring a consent banner — but full transparency below.

CookiePurposeTypeExpires
session Keeps you signed in after Google sign-in Strictly necessary 7 days

This cookie is httpOnly (inaccessible to JavaScript on the page), Secure (sent only over HTTPS), and SameSite=Lax. It contains only a signed token identifying your session — no tracking data.

Local storage

Your browser's localStorage is used for one thing: remembering your language preference (English/French) between visits. This never leaves your browser and is not a cookie.

Third-party cookies

None are set by this site. Google's own sign-in script (Google Identity Services) may set its own cookies under Google's domain as part of the sign-in flow — those are governed by Google's own cookie policy, not ours.
