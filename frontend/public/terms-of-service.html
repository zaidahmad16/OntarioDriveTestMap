Terms of Service

Last updated September 21, 2026

What this site is

OntarioDriveTestMap is a free, community-maintained map of G/G2 DriveTest routes across Ontario. Route data comes from real GPS traces, hand-transcribed test videos, and user-submitted reports — each labeled honestly by confidence tier (Confirmed, Inferred, or an Unrouted gap), never presented as more certain than the underlying evidence supports.

Not an official source. OntarioDriveTestMap is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected to DriveTest, Serco Canada Inc., or the Ontario Ministry of Transportation. Actual test routes are chosen by your examiner and can vary. Nothing on this site guarantees the route you'll be tested on.

Community content

Forum posts, discussion posts, comments, photos, and route submissions are written by users, not by us. By posting, you agree that:

  • You won't post anything illegal, harassing, defamatory, or that violates someone else's privacy
  • You have the right to share any photo or content you upload
  • Route/turn submissions reflect your own genuine experience, not guesses presented as fact
  • Content you post publicly (forum/discussion posts, comments, submitted routes) may remain visible even after you delete your account, attributed only to an anonymous placeholder — see the Privacy Policy

Posts are moderated automatically (a blocked-word filter, rate limits, and community voting/reporting that auto-hides low-scored or heavily reported content) — there is no human review queue and no paid moderation staff.

Route confidence, not verification

A route or turn marked "Confirmed" means multiple independent sources corroborate it — it does not mean an official body verified it, and it does not mean your examiner will drive that exact route. "Predicted" sections are inferred from partial evidence. Community votes/upvotes on a discussion post never change a route's confidence tier — those are two separate systems, and popularity of a post is not evidence of route accuracy.

No cost, no purchases

OntarioDriveTestMap is free to use. There is no payment system, no subscription, and nothing to buy — so there is no refund policy, because nothing is ever charged.

Account termination

We may suspend or remove an account that repeatedly violates these terms (spam, harassment, posting illegal content, circumventing moderation). You can delete your own account at any time from the account menu in the site header.

No warranty

This site is provided "as is," maintained by a single independent developer/community, with no guarantee of uptime, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose. Use route information as a study aid, not a guarantee of what you'll be tested on.
